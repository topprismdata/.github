# TopPrism

### Decision intelligence for the physical business world — and Native AI systems that compound organizational capability.

TopPrism builds two compounding intelligence loops.

---

## 1. Customer Decision Intelligence

We model real business operations and turn complex states, constraints and objectives into computable decisions.

```text
Business World
     ↓
World Model
     ↓
Decision Engine
     ↓
Execution
     ↓
Outcome & Feedback
     └──────────────→ better next decision
```

**Representative capabilities**

- **Business World Modeling** — entities, spatial context, demand, resources, relations and constraints.
- **Decision Engines** — market design, territory, visit planning, routing, warehouse decisions and other optimization problems.
- **Open Evidence** — anonymized operational studies, public real-world datasets, benchmarks and reproducible research.

### Explore

- `visit-scheduling-optimizer` — periodic field-sales visit planning.
- `market-partition` — deterministic spatial partitioning with agentic interpretation and verification.
- `bge-entity-match` — entity resolution for canonical business objects.
- `agentic-warehouse-engine` — decision-science research on dynamic warehouse reconfiguration.

---

## 2. Native AI Organization

We also use AI to improve how TopPrism itself learns and works.

```text
Project
  ↓
Experience
  ↓
Knowledge
  ↓
Reusable Skill
  ↓
Better Agent
  ↓
Next Project starts from a higher baseline
```

**Representative capabilities**

- `cultivating-ml-agent` — an ML agent that compounds capability across projects.
- `agent-nurture-framework` — a general methodology for project-driven agent capability development.
- `skill-tester` — a quality and trigger-evaluation gate for reusable agent skills.
- `notebook-knowledge-distillation` — source-to-skill knowledge distillation with validation.
- `topprismwiki` — evidence-governed knowledge infrastructure for enterprise AI agents.

## How to read our repositories

Every original TopPrism repository is classified by:

**Purpose** — Customer Decision / World Model / Native AI / Decision Science / Learning Project

**Maturity** — Production / Applied / Real-data Validated / Research / Internal Utility / Learning

**Evidence** — Customer / Operational Data / Public Real-world Data / Benchmark / Internal Evaluation

We deliberately separate **technical evidence** from **commercial maturity**. A real-data research result is not presented as a production deployment, and an internal evaluation is not presented as an external benchmark.
