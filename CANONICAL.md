# TopPrism canonical terminology

> **Language / 语言:** English primary · 中文定义 included below。

This page is the shared vocabulary for TopPrism's website, GitHub profiles and
repository READMEs. It is a terminology guide, not a claim that every public
repository is one deployed product.

## Entity

**TopPrism（棱镜极智能）** builds AI-powered decision intelligence for
consumer-goods businesses. It turns physical-world data, business knowledge,
objectives and operational constraints into explainable insights,
recommendations and decisions that people can review and act on.

**棱镜极智能**为消费品企业提供 AI 决策智能，将物理世界数据、业务知识、经营目标与约束，
转化为可解释、可复核、可执行的洞察、建议与决策。

## Core terms

### Customer Decision Intelligence

The customer-facing capability: understand operational reality, explain what
it means with evidence and limits, form alternatives or recommendations, and
support human-approved decisions and action.

中文：面向消费品企业的决策智能，依次完成**理解现实、解释意义、形成方案、支持决策与行动**。

### Business World Model

A governed representation of business entities, relationships, spatial
context, demand, resources and constraints. It is a semantic and evidence
layer; it is not automatically a source of truth for every business.

中文：对业务实体、关系、空间环境、需求、资源和约束进行受治理的表达，是语义与证据层，
不等同于未经审核的“全局真相”。

### Native AI

TopPrism's internal learning loop for turning project experience into
evaluated, governed and reusable organizational capability. It is not a claim
that every repository is an autonomous agent runtime.

中文：把项目经验转化为经过评测、治理并可复用的组织能力；不表示所有仓库都是自治 Agent
运行时。

### Spatial intelligence

A capability within the Business World Model: spatial data quality,
boundaries, market geometry, corridors and location-aware constraints. It is
an important capability, but it is not the company-level name for TopPrism.

中文：业务世界模型中的一组能力，包括空间数据质量、边界、市场几何、商业街区和位置约束；
它不是 TopPrism 的公司级名称。

## Boundary language

- Use **Customer Decision Intelligence** for the customer-facing portfolio.
- Use **Business World Model** for the semantic, entity and spatial foundation.
- Use **Native AI** for the internal capability-formation loop.
- Describe `spatial-decision-intelligence` by its repository evidence and
  current scope; do not use it as the umbrella name for TopPrism.
- Do not describe a research result as a production deployment, or an internal
  evaluation as an external benchmark.
